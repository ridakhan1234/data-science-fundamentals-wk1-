# My First Month as a Data Analyst at Google Pay

Starting my journey as a Data Analyst at Google Pay, my first month was focused on understanding the business, working with real-world data, and learning how data supports better decision-making.

* Gained an understanding of **digital payments, UPI transactions, refunds, and transaction life cycles**.
* Explored organizational databases and learned how transaction and customer-related data is structured.
* Used **SQL** to extract, filter, aggregate, and analyze transaction data based on business requirements.
* Used **Python and Pandas** for data cleaning, transformation, and exploratory data analysis.
* Identified and handled **missing values, duplicate records, inconsistencies, and unusual transaction patterns**.
* Analyzed key business metrics such as **transaction volume, success rate, failure rate, and average transaction value**.
* Created **data visualizations and dashboards** to communicate trends and patterns clearly.
* Collaborated with senior analysts and business teams to understand business problems and translate them into analytical questions.
* Prepared analytical reports that highlighted important trends and provided **actionable insights** for decision-making.
* By the end of the month, I gained practical experience in the complete analytics workflow — **from understanding raw data to delivering meaningful business insights**.

### Key Skills Used

**SQL • Python • Pandas • Data Cleaning • Exploratory Data Analysis • Data Visualization • Dashboarding • Business Analytics • Problem Solving**
